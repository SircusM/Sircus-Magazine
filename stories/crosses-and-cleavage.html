Issue 13 · Opulence & Desire

Crosses& Cleavage

On the cross as a fashion accessory, and what happens when conviction becomes optics.

It is about optics.The cross no longer testifies to conviction,but to the intention to be seen…

For most of its history, the cross was worn to be read. It hung at the throat as a profession of faith, modest in scale and plain in meaning. It now arrives in sterling and pavé, sized for the camera and set against bare skin, and it is read very differently. Crosses & Cleavage follows the symbol from the sanctuary to the spotlight.

It examines what the cross sheds along the way. The question at its center is not whether the cross belongs in fashion. It is a harder one: once a sacred sign is chosen for the way it frames a neckline, whom is it addressing, and what does the wearer surrender in the exchange?
